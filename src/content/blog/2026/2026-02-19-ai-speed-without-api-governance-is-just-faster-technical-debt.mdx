AI speed without API governance is just faster technical debt.

ROI of Integration Governance is where enterprise teams usually discover whether architecture is truly operational or just conceptual.

In practice, the bottleneck is rarely a single platform. It is the integration boundary between CRM, order, fulfillment, data, and policy layers.

Three execution points that matter:
- Define contract-first integrations with explicit ownership and versioning.
- Enforce runtime controls (authZ, throttling, traceability) where flows cross domains.
- Measure outcomes using lead time, reliability, reuse, and risk reduction, not component count.

The teams that win are not the ones with fewer systems. They are the ones with better-governed interactions between systems.
