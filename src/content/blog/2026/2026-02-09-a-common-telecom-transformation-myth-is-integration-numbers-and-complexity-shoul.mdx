A common telecom transformation myth is: integration numbers and complexity should decline as maturity increases.

Reality: integrations usually increase before they optimize.

Why is that?
Stage 1 - Modernization: legacy and cloud coexist, then core API enablement starts.

Stage 2 - Scale: more B2C/B2B/app channels, partner ecosystem growth, and event/data platform expansion.

Stage 3 - AI at Scale: model/data integrations, process and agent orchestration, and stronger governance/security/compliance controls.

So the target state is not fewer integrations.
The target state is better-managed integrations.

The KPI shift should be explicit:
- Reuse
- Lead time
- Reliability
- ROI

If leadership only asks, “How many integrations do we have?”, they can optimize for the wrong outcome.

The better board question is: “How well-governed and reusable are our integrations, and what business speed do they unlock?”
