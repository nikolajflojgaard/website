Agentic delivery (including OpenClaw-style orchestration) can speed up CRM innovation.

But in enterprise CRM, the hardest part is not UI generation. It is controlled execution.

In core CRM transaction flows, teams still need:
- strict role and field-level access
- full audit trails and traceability
- deterministic process behavior
- safe cross-system dependencies

That is why agentic adoption often shifts effort to the integration platform:
- API contracts, versioning, and policy enforcement
- rate limits/quotas and traffic shaping
- identity propagation and authorization
- retries, idempotency, and incident handling
- observability and lineage

So the practical architecture pattern is:
Agents at the edge. Integration governance at the core.

The key question for enterprise leaders is not:
“Can agents build CRM features faster?”
It's:
“Can our integration platform govern that new speed safely and at scale?”
