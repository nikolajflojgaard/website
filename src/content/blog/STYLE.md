# Blog writing default

Default to **human, opinionated, specific writing**.

Use this as the baseline for new posts and imported/article rewrites:

- write like a smart person talking to other smart people
- prefer concrete examples over vague summaries
- keep sentences natural; vary rhythm and length
- cut filler, hype, and generic AI-sounding transitions
- have a point of view instead of pretending to be neutral on everything
- choose clarity over polish when the two conflict
- if a line sounds like LinkedIn sludge, rewrite it

Quick self-check before publishing:

- Would a real person say this out loud?
- Is there at least one concrete claim, example, or observation per section?
- Did we remove obvious AI crutches like "in today's fast-paced landscape", "delve", "unlock", or empty bullet spam?
- Does the piece sound like Nikolaj, not a content machine?
