  <p class="text-lg leading-8 text-foreground">I work at the intersection of enterprise architecture, integration, automation, and AI.</p>
  <p>By day, I’m a Senior Integration Architect at TDC NET. The job is not just to make systems connect. It is to make them hold up under real business pressure. That means architecture that scales, integrations that survive messy reality, and automation that creates actual leverage instead of just moving complexity around.</p>
  <p>A lot of my attention is on agentic workflows and AI. Not the hype version, the practical version, what can actually be trusted, governed, integrated, and used to improve how work gets done across systems, teams, and customer operations.</p>
  <p>I’m generally skeptical of technology theatre. Fancy language does not matter much if the workflow is sloppy, the architecture is weak, or the thing falls apart in production.</p>
  <p>I build, test, write, and share ideas around enterprise architecture, APIs, workflow design, agentic systems, autonomy, and software that solves real problems.</p>
