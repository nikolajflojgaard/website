## AI Integration Co-Pilot (from 0 to first pilot)

This is the business I’m building toward:

**An AI Integration Co-Pilot for mid-market operations teams** that helps companies automate cross-system workflows safely (CRM, support, order, fulfillment, finance, policy), with governance and measurable outcomes.

### Problem

Most teams can demo AI quickly, but fail in production due to:
- unclear integration ownership
- weak policy/guardrails
- missing operational reliability

### Offer (MVP)

- Integration baseline mapping (current systems + bottlenecks)
- 2-3 high-value agentic workflow playbooks
- policy guardrails (auth, access, escalation)
- outcome dashboard (lead time, reliability, automation coverage)

### ICP

- Mid-market companies with 50-500 employees
- Ops-heavy environments (telecom, services, B2B operations)
- Existing SaaS stack, but fragmented execution

### 30-day execution plan

#### Week 1 — Foundation
- Define one crystal-clear offer and pricing hypothesis
- Build landing narrative + outreach one-pager
- Create 2 packaged pilot options (Lite / Pro)

#### Week 2 — Proof assets
- Build one end-to-end demo workflow
- Record short walkthrough (3-5 min)
- Prepare KPI baseline template for prospects

#### Week 3 — Pipeline
- Run founder-led outreach to 30 qualified leads
- Book 8 discovery calls
- Qualify for urgency + systems complexity

#### Week 4 — Close first pilot
- Convert 1-2 pilots with scoped 4-week engagement
- Ship first measurable workflow improvement
- Capture case study and testimonials

### Target outcome

**First paying pilot within 30 days**, then productize repeatable components into SaaS modules.
